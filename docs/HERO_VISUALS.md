# Semantic hero visuals

Every main public page opens with its text and an image that shows what the
page is about, on alternating sides. The component is
`components/ui/PageHeroVisual.tsx` (server component; the image itself is
`components/ui/HeroImage.tsx`), the images and their sides are configured in
`data/heroVisuals.ts`, their alt texts (RU/KZ/EN) in
`data/i18n/heroVisuals.ts`.

## Current state: temporary mockups

No image generator was available, so the pages use **temporary vector UI
mockups** (`public/images/hero/*.svg`, 5–8 KB each): no text inside (the same
image for every language), no client logos, no invented cases. They are
generated by `frontend/scripts/hero-svgs.py` (Python 3, no dependencies; from
`frontend/`: `python3 scripts/hero-svgs.py`, writes `public/images/hero/`) so
they stay one series; edit the script, not the SVGs. They are meant for approval
of the layout and the meaning of each image, and to be replaced by the final
illustrations below.

## Final illustrations: prompts

Common style for every image (prepend to each prompt):

> Premium tech illustration, isometric-free 3/4 front view, glassmorphism UI
> panels floating in dark space, deep navy background #070B17 fading to
> fully transparent at the edges, soft violet (#7C3AED) and blue (#2563EB)
> rim lighting with cyan (#06B6D4) accents, subtle glow, clean minimal
> composition with generous empty space, rounded corners, thin luminous
> borders, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no
> people's faces, 1280×1040 px, PNG with transparent background.

Ready images: PNG (alpha) or WebP, 1280×1040 (2× the 640×520 box), each
under ~250 KB before optimization; next/image serves them as AVIF or WebP
(`next.config.ts images.formats`) at the sizes the layout needs. If a
generator cannot produce transparency, use the #070B17 background with
edges fading into it, and check the light theme.

## Replacing an image

1. Put the file in `public/images/hero/` (e.g. `pricing.webp`).
2. In `data/heroVisuals.ts` set its `src`, `width` and `height` (the
   intrinsic size; keep the 640:520 ratio or adjust both).
3. Update the alt text in `data/i18n/heroVisuals.ts` if the content differs.

A new page: add a key to `HeroVisualKey`, an entry with its side and alt
texts in three languages, and render `<PageHeroVisual … image={heroVisuals.key} alt={heroVisualAlts[locale].key} />`.

## Performance and SEO

- `next/image` with intrinsic width/height: the box is reserved, no layout
  shift (CLS 0.000 on every page, 1440 and 375 px).
- Above the fold: `loading="eager"` and `fetchPriority="high"` (Next.js 16:
  `priority` is deprecated; `preload` is not used because the LCP element is
  the heading on phones and the image on wide screens).
- `sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 560px, (min-width: 500px) 460px, 92vw"`.
- Floating motion only under `motion-safe` (and the global
  `prefers-reduced-motion` rule).
- Meaningful alt texts in three languages; the glows are `aria-hidden`.
- Titles, descriptions, canonical, hreflang, robots, sitemap: unchanged.
